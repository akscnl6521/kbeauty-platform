Create a clear, beginner-friendly skincare guide that explains two different cleansing routines and the correct order of skincare products for morning and evening.

The guide should include the following sections:

1. Morning Cleansing Method

* Explain how to cleanse the face in the morning.
* Mention that people with dry or sensitive skin may use lukewarm water only or a very gentle cleanser.
* Explain that people with oily skin may use a mild, low-pH facial cleanser.
* Emphasize using lukewarm water and avoiding harsh scrubbing.

2. Morning Skincare Product Order
   Present the products in the correct order:

Cleanser → Toner → Essence → Serum or Ampoule → Eye Cream → Lotion or Emulsion → Moisturizer → Sunscreen → Makeup

Explain that skincare products should generally be applied from the lightest, most watery texture to the thickest texture.

Mention that sunscreen must always be the final skincare step before makeup.

3. Evening Cleansing Method

* Explain how to cleanse the face at night.
* If sunscreen or makeup was worn, recommend double cleansing:
  Cleansing Oil, Cleansing Balm, or Micellar Water → Gentle Facial Cleanser
* If no makeup or water-resistant sunscreen was worn, explain that one gentle cleanse may be sufficient.
* Emphasize removing makeup, sunscreen, excess oil, dust, and pollution without over-cleansing the skin.

4. Evening Skincare Product Order
   Present the products in the correct order:

Makeup Remover or Oil Cleanser → Facial Cleanser → Toner → Essence → Serum or Ampoule → Treatment Product → Eye Cream → Lotion or Emulsion → Moisturizer → Face Oil or Sleeping Mask

Explain that face oil and sleeping masks are optional and should usually be applied as the final step.

5. Active Ingredient Guidance
   Briefly explain where treatment products should be used in the routine.

Include examples such as:

* Retinol
* AHA or BHA exfoliants
* Acne treatment products
* Brightening products
* Soothing or hydrating serums

Mention that retinol and strong exfoliating acids should not automatically be used together on the same night because they may cause irritation.

6. Skin-Type Adjustments
   Provide simple recommendations for:

* Dry skin
* Oily skin
* Combination skin
* Sensitive skin
* Acne-prone skin

Keep the explanation practical, medically cautious, and easy to understand. Avoid making guaranteed medical claims.

Use short sections, clear headings, and simple step-by-step explanations. Include both the full routine and a simplified routine for beginners.
